What is Prepfly?

Prepfly is a full-stack PTE Academic preparation platform built for both individual students who want to practice at their own pace and coaching centres that need to manage batches of students under one roof. The platform combines AI-powered feedback, gamified progress tracking, and comprehensive analytics — without the high monthly fees of traditional prep platforms.

Section 2

Student Features

2.1 Practice Engine

🏋️ Core Practice
🗂️

Practice by Section & Type

Browse every PTE question type organised by skill — Speaking, Writing, Reading, and Listening. See exactly how many questions are available, the time limit per question, and jump straight into practice with one click. Filter by topic, difficulty, or source (platform-wide or centre-specific).

🎯

AI-Powered Scoring for Speaking & Writing AI

After each speaking response, Prepfly's AI evaluates your answer against PTE band descriptors and gives you a score out of 90 — the same scale as the real exam. Writing responses receive detailed feedback on content, grammar, structure, and vocabulary. Temperature-tuned at 0.2 with a fixed seed for low variance across repeated attempts.

✅

Objective Auto-Scoring (Server-Side)

MCQ, Reorder Paragraphs, Fill in the Blanks, Dictation, and Highlight Incorrect Words are scored server-side in milliseconds. Answer keys are never sent to the browser — preventing cheating while keeping latency low. Tolerant matching for Fill-in-the-Blanks handles punctuation, case, and surrounding spaces.

🔄

Re-Try & Score Comparison

After any attempt, hit "Try Again" to redo the same question. Prepfly automatically shows you the delta — whether your new score is higher or lower than your last attempt — with a green "Score improved!" banner and an up/down arrow indicator. A trend chart tracks all your scores over time for every question.

🔖

Question Flagging & Bookmarking

Flag any question as Weak, Review Again, or Strong. Your flags are saved and accessible from a dedicated "My Flags" page so you can drill down on the exact questions that need attention without hunting through the question bank.

⚠️

Question Reporting

Spot a wrong answer, broken audio, or a typo? Report it in one tap. Reports are routed to admins with full context (question ID, user ID, issue type) so the content team can fix problems quickly.

2.2 Mock Tests

📝 Full & Sectional Mock Tests
🏛️

Real Exam Simulation

Full-length mock tests mirror the actual PTE structure — 28 Speaking, 3 Writing, 11 Reading, and 14 Listening questions — with authentic time limits per section. The interface prevents skipping ahead between sections, just like the real exam.

📋

Sectional Tests

Prepare for a specific skill without committing to a 3-hour session. Sectional mock tests let you focus on your weakest area, time-boxed and scored exactly like the full test.

📊

Detailed Score Report

After completing a test, a full report breaks down your score by section and question type, shows which questions you answered vs. left pending, and provides the honest "Answered + pending (counted as 0)" calculation — no inflated scores. Compare against your previous mock test attempts at a glance.

🏫

Centre-Assigned Tests

Your coaching centre can assign specific mock tests to your batch. These appear in a separate "Assigned" tab so you never miss a batch exam. Centre teachers track completion rates and scores across all students.

Coaching Centre Features

Built for How Centres Actually Work

Prepfly's centre tools handle the full student lifecycle — from onboarding to renewal — and give teachers real-time visibility into every student's progress. No spreadsheets, no manual tracking, no guesswork.

3.1 Student Management

👥 Student Lifecycle Management
📧

Invite Students by Email

Send a personalised email invitation directly from the dashboard. The student receives a link that auto-connects their account to your centre upon registration. No manual coordination needed.

📤

Bulk CSV Import New

Upload a CSV file with student names, emails, and phone numbers to onboard up to 100 students at once. Existing users are linked and granted a seat automatically; new emails receive a registration invitation. A results summary shows how many were imported, invited, skipped (already enrolled), or errored — with per-row error details.

Generate a shareable invite link or QR code. Share in your WhatsApp group or class — students who register via this link are automatically enrolled in your centre. One-click copy from the dashboard.

Renew one student's seat in two clicks, or select multiple students and renew them all at once. The system automatically detects students expiring in the next 7 or 30 days and highlights them with a filter chip — so no one falls through the cracks.

📥

Export Student Data (CSV)

Download a complete student export with names, emails, seat status, batch assignments, total attempts, and mock tests completed. Useful for fee tracking, reporting, and backup.

💺

Seat-Usage Gauge

A live "45 / 50 seats used" gauge shows exactly how close you are to your plan limit. Clearly shows cost per seat and plan tier so there are no billing surprises when adding new students.

3.2 Batch & Group Management

🗂️ Batches & Class Groups
📦

Create & Manage Batches

Organise students into batches (e.g. "Morning Batch," "Weekend Intensive"). Create unlimited batches, assign students, and view batch composition. Delete batches when a term ends without affecting student data.

Batch Progress Dashboard

Push a specific mock test to an entire batch with one click. All batch members see the assigned test in their "Assigned" tab and completion status is tracked in real time on the batch progress page.

Scoring Methodology

Objective questions (MCQ, Reorder, Fill-in-the-Blanks, Dictation, Highlight Incorrect Words) are scored server-side with tolerant matching — handling case differences, surrounding punctuation, and whitespace — so partially-correct answers are scored fairly, not harshly.

Subjective questions (Speaking, Writing) are evaluated by an AI model calibrated against PTE band descriptors with example responses at each score level. Low temperature (0.2) and a fixed seed ensure consistent scoring — your score reflects your answer quality, not random variation.

About the Platforms Compared

🇦🇺

E2 Language

Australia-based, widely used globally. Strong video lesson library and AI speaking feedback. However, USD-only pricing makes it expensive for Indian students (~₹2,000–4,000/month). No B2B centre tools. Primarily targets individual self-study students.

🇨🇳

APEUNI

China-origin platform, popular for AI scoring accuracy on Speaking. Strong prediction question library. Priced in USD/AUD, interface partially translated, and no coaching centre management layer. Used mainly in Australia-based centres informally.

🌐

PTE Magic

Online-only platform with a reasonable question bank. Limited AI feedback, mostly rule-based. No centre tools, no student management, and limited progress analytics. Reasonable pricing for individuals but missing advanced features.

🏛️

Pearson Practice App

The official Pearson PTE practice app. Highest content authenticity (official questions) but very limited question count, no AI feedback, no centre management, no community features. Best used as a final-week supplement, not a primary prep tool.
