## What is PTE Master?

PTE Master is an AI-powered PTE Academic practice platform built specifically for coaching centres. Your students can:

- Practice all 20+ PTE question types (Speaking, Writing, Reading, Listening)
- Take full mock tests with timer and scoring
- Get AI-powered feedback on Speaking and Writing
- Track their progress with detailed analytics
- Access everything from phone, tablet, or computer

You (as Centre Admin) can:
- See all enrolled students and their progress
- Manage batches (Morning Batch, Evening Batch, etc.)
- Add your own custom questions
- View analytics — who is practicing, who is not
- Get student feedback

## How Students Use the Platform

### Practicing

1. Student logs in → sees **Dashboard** with 4 sections:
   - Speaking (6 question types)
   - Writing (2 question types)
   - Reading (5 question types)
   - Listening (7 question types)

2. Click any section → see all question types with descriptions

3. Click a question type → practice questions one by one:
   - See the question
   - Answer it (type, speak, select options)
   - Get instant feedback + correct answer
   - Move to next question

### Taking a Mock Test

1. Click **"Mock Test"** in sidebar
2. Click **"Start New Mock Test"**
3. The system automatically selects random questions for all 4 sections
4. Timer starts — student answers each question
5. After finishing → click **"Finish Test"**
6. See full scorecard: Speaking / Writing / Reading / Listening / Overall

### Checking Progress

Students can see:
- Practice streak (consecutive days)
- Total questions done
- Average score
- Section-wise performance
- Weak areas to focus on
- Strong areas

## How to Install on Phone (Like an App)

Students don't need to download from Play Store. They can install the web app directly:

### Android (Chrome):

1. Open the website in **Chrome**
2. Tap the **3 dots menu** (top right)
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**
4. Tap **"Install"**
5. The PTE Master icon will appear on the home screen

### iPhone (Safari):

1. Open the website in **Safari**
2. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow at bottom)
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**
5. The PTE Master icon will appear on the home screen

### Windows/Mac (Chrome):

1. Open the website in **Chrome**
2. Click the **install icon** in the address bar (right side)
3. Click **"Install"**
4. The app will open in its own window

After installing, it works like a native app — full screen, fast, works offline for some features.

## Adding Custom Questions

You can add your own practice questions:

1. Go to **Questions** in sidebar
2. Click **"Add Question"**
3. Select:
   - **Section:** Speaking / Writing / Reading / Listening
   - **Type:** e.g., Read Aloud, Write Essay, MCQ, etc.
   - **Difficulty:** Easy / Medium / Hard
4. Fill in the question content
5. Add model answer (optional) and explanation (optional)
6. Click **"Save Question"**

Your custom questions will be available **only to your centre's students** — not visible to other centres.

## AI Scoring — How It Works

For **Speaking** questions (Read Aloud, Repeat Sentence, etc.):
- Student records their voice
- AI analyzes: pronunciation, fluency, content
- Gives a score out of 90 + specific feedback
- Shows what to improve

For **Writing** questions (Essay, Summarize Written Text):
- Student types their response
- AI checks: grammar, spelling, content, structure, vocabulary
- Gives a score out of 90 + corrections
- Shows specific grammar/spelling errors

**Note:** AI scoring requires an OpenAI API key to be configured. During initial setup, this will be provided.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does it work on slow internet?**
A: Yes. The web app is lightweight (~100KB). Once installed as PWA, some pages work offline too.

**Q: Can students use it on their phone?**
A: Yes. It's fully responsive — works on any screen size. They can install it like an app from the browser.

**Q: How many students can register?**
A: Unlimited during the free beta period.

**Q: Can other coaching centres see our questions?**
A: No. Custom questions you add are visible only to your centre's students. The shared question bank is available to everyone.

**Q: What happens after the free beta period?**
A: We'll introduce pricing plans. You'll have the option to choose a plan based on your student count. All existing data will be preserved.

**Q: Can students practice without internet?**
A: Reading and some listening questions can work offline once the page is cached. Speaking and AI scoring require internet.
